Key Findings

The top 10 products bring in 62.7% of Shopify revenue and the Dwellings of Eldervale line alone brings in 47.6%.

Meta sent 30% of site traffic but converted at 0.22%, less than half of the 0.50% rate of free Organic Search traffic.

The 92.3% cart abandonment rate drops to 26.4% once dates are matched and bots are removed. High shipping costs are the most likely real cause.

Recommendations

  1. Print heavy on the top 10 products above, led by the Dwellings of Eldervale family, and keep the base game deeply stocked so its add-ons can sell.
  2. Stop repeating five Meta campaigns that spent $24,654 for no recorded results or over $100 per purchase, and move that money to email, BoardGameGeek, and a purchase-focused retest of our best ad.
  3. Show shipping estimates earlier for international shoppers (average $35.80 per cart) and add a US free-shipping threshold around $50.
